## Conditional issues affecting particular comparisons

- **Protection history or station identity:** resolve a discrepancy if it changes treatment assignment or the baseline window; otherwise retain the dated evidence and disclose what is unknown. Network dates are not automatically first protection dates.
- **Missing survey-depth metadata:** KFM/LTER count files do not provide compatible measured depths. Do not require invented numbers. Decide what can be supported using documented fixed stations/protocols and what must remain limited or secondary.
- **LECHUZA coordinates:** resolve before any distance- or boundary-based selection using those coordinates. The conflict does not automatically invalidate a named-station count history, but cannot be hidden in a distance claim.
- **Repeated values:** verify full sampling keys where an actual join or deduplication depends on them. The inventory's selected-field duplicate screen alone does not establish duplicate organisms or invalid records.

## Items that need not hold up the numerical freeze

- **Trailer and Willows:** keep unassigned and outside the primary analysis. Ask the mapping custodian only if there is a reason to pursue those labels; do not add them speculatively.
- **Unrecovered Naples nMDS and individual historical matching notes:** contributor evidence supports documenting the existing choices and their limitations. These records are not needed to pretend that habitat equivalence has been proved; it has not. New contrary evidence would warrant reassessment.
- **Unavailable full KFM predator sizes:** retain density-only histories rather than waiting indefinitely or borrowing an unverified size distribution.
- **Exact dates absent from size records:** do not claim visit matching. Declare the defensible pooling level and its limitations; if that is inadequate for a primary biomass comparison, keep that comparison secondary.
- **Satellite historical clustering details:** use verified saved masks rather than rerunning stochastic clustering. Historical causal-comparability limits remain in interpretation.
