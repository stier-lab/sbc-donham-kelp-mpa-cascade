# Joint density model: checks of the recommended structure

Generated by `code/joint_model/report_r.py`, 2026-11-10. Structure: random-walk year effect shared by all stations plus a random-walk deviation per region (three regions: northern Channel Islands, southern islands, mainland), first-increment prior Normal(0, 1.2), negative-binomial counts. Background: `REPORT.md` (stage-1 gates) and `REPORT_structure_experiments.md` (why this structure).

Review only. The structure is selected with flags; the default in `model.py` is still the stage-1 model and nothing is adopted for the paper. No specification, prior, station or cohort was chosen by the direction or significance of a result.

## Bottom line

Under this structure the joint model is usable as a cross-check on the paper's pipeline, with limits that should be stated every time it is shown.

- **Lobster density rises inside reserves.** E10 +1.74 [+1.05, +2.44]. Robust to seed, prior, every sensitivity and both placebos. Simulation says the estimate errs low by about 0.16.
- **Purple urchin and red urchin density probably fall.** E10 -0.95 [-1.86, -0.01] and -0.63 [-1.23, -0.06]. About 96% of each posterior is below zero, but both intervals only just exclude it; for purple urchin a second seed puts the upper end at zero and leaving out Harris Point moves it to +0.05. Under the `piecewise` prior neither excludes zero.
- **Sheephead density shows no detectable response.** E10 +0.21 [-0.08, +0.50], and the pre-2003 time placebo fails for sheephead.

Three checks still fail by the rules set in the stage-1 report. None is as serious as it was: calibration misses the coverage floor of 0.85 in two cells at 0.84, the sheephead placebos fail for reasons that are understood, and the urchin count layer still fails its predictive checks without affecting the estimate. I have not loosened any rule to get a pass.

Power for a moderate effect (a 48% decline per decade) is 34%, so a null result from this model on an effect of that size means little. `piecewise` almost never gives a false positive and under-covers moderate and large urchin effects (0.65 and 0.74); it should not be presented as a bound.

## Not verified

- One shared trend against regional trends: still an ecological judgment (see `REPORT_structure_experiments.md`). Under one shared trend lobster is about +1.2 and red urchin about -0.8.
- The trend simulations use one trend size (0.8 per decade).
- Why the two Point Dume reference stations appear to rise in lobster and sheephead relative to the mainland trend was not examined, and the real Point Dume estimates were not rerun with other controls.
- The kelp space placebo (negative under every structure, with and without Point Dume) is unexplained. Kelp was run on counts only: no biomass, no Landsat, no sensitivities.
- The urchin count-layer misfit is unresolved.
- The purple urchin time placebo leans negative; three MPAs and seven stations cannot say whether that is real.
- The paper's pipeline was not rerun or compared number for number.
- Nothing is adopted. The default in `model.py` is still the stage-1 model, and no stage 2 (multi-taxon) or biomass work was started.
