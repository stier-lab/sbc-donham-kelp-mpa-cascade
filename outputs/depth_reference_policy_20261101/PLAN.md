# Depth/reference recommendation audit

Review only. No abundance slopes or pooled effects will be fitted or used to choose the recommendation in this task. Preserve expert exclusions and all previous artifacts.

Read original coauthor messages and published monitoring methods. Distinguish confirmed ecological advice, tentative memory, inherited mapping and analyst-created tolerances. Do not nominate a single new reference merely because it maximizes survey years.

Inspect raw dated transect depth values, missing/conflicting values, calendar year versus provider survey_year and original station coordinates. Units are actual station/date/zone/transect events, not species rows. For conflicting depths retain min/max and use the median of unique recorded values descriptively; do not claim an exact physical location. The monitoring protocol has non-fixed transects. Numeric transect IDs are not persistent locations across years.

Evaluate all inherited eligible reference combinations at the five affected MPAs. Keep original INNER and MID strata distinct; never substitute DEEP for missing shallow surveys. Measured-depth support is the overlap of protected and reference recorded transect-center ranges within each MPA/zone over the post record. This is common observed support, not habitat certification. Compare excluding unknown/out-of-support depths versus retaining the original strata with unknown depths flagged.

Match same calendar year AND same provider survey_year; retain true observation dates for analysis time. Match visits within zone using minimum absolute date separation; all candidate references remain available. Compare 30,60,90 days and unrestricted timing; these are analyst tolerances, not biologically established thresholds. Require at least two usable transects per station/date/zone (protocol default), both INNER and MID in a pair-year, and at least five paired post years spanning five elapsed years for a primary-recovery candidate. Also record three-year support as sensitivity. All calendar months are eligible; provider campaign alignment and actual-date matching replace arbitrary month exclusion. Equal-distance ties resolved by dates/IDs only, never abundance.

Retain every pair satisfying a rule, report shared stations and role-specific membership, and do not count pairs as independent MPAs. No new model estimates claimed. A primary implementation must deduplicate repeated use of survey observations, account for station/stratum composition and shared references, and standardize predicted responses over common observed depth support. Data sufficiency/rank checks are distinct from validating that implementation.

The candidate recommendation is the measured-common-depth, same-calendar/survey-year, <=90-day, five-year/five-span design, with <=60-day and zone-only/missing-depth sensitivities. Justification for90days is a transparent quarter-year operational tolerance balancing the annual sampling design with date matching, not empirical seasonal equivalence or favorable biological results. Reassess if original source evidence contradicts it; preserve the audit of all alternatives.

## Coverage-stage refinement (before any outcome refit)

Hard-trimming individual transects to the empirical protected/reference range intersection caused complete-pair losses when one of the two transects was slightly outside a sample minimum/maximum. Reject that trim as the primary policy: sample extrema are not biological exclusion limits. Retain it as Common_depth sensitivity.

The recommended candidate is Measured_shallow: known positive recorded transect-center depth <=16.25m, original INNER/MID strata, at least2usable transects per visit, overlapping paired-record depth ranges within each stratum, <=90days and >=5pairedyears spanning5years. 16.25m is an analyst-defined midpoint between the protocol's12.5m middle and20m deep targets, delimiting the two shallower strata, not a provider-prescribed cutoff. Extreme nominal INNER/MID depths are flagged separately, not declared data-entry errors. Retain raw measurements. Prediction standardization must stay within paired observed depth support and adjust station/stratum and measured depth; this audit does not claim to have fit that model. Unknown depth is a sensitivity-only inclusion, never an imputed measurement. Same-campaign matching requires both zones to belong to one campaign; multiple campaigns in a calendar year are resolved by tightest joint date gap, then campaign ID. Coordinates conflicting across mappings are left unknown, not used to choose pairs.
