# Emily biomass bridge: first common-year diagnostic

November 3, 2026. Review only; no paper or primary-model changes.

This check holds the current PISCO red- and purple-urchin biomass comparison roster, observed count units, zones, visits, years, aggregation order and ratio offsets fixed. It changes only the size pool used to estimate mass: current visit-supported biomass versus an archived-code-style pool grouped by comparison, protection role, year and species. Both calculations use the same nonlinear size-to-mass equations. The reconstructed pool reads Emily's archived combined size file and physical-site crosswalk, applies the listed researcher site exclusions and 25 mm urchin threshold, and pools by comparison, role, year and species as her code does. It does not replay every downstream step of her original model or the exact 1,000 size draws. The archived source hashes are saved alongside the results.

There are 294 candidate comparison-years in 20 histories; 30 comparison-years in six histories have both protected and reference biomass under both calculations. Those are the **same 30 years** for every point-estimate comparison. Six histories have at least three matched post years for a descriptive slope. Five have at least four for the uncertainty diagnostic. These are very small and selected subsets, not a pooled manuscript cohort.

Across the six descriptive post slopes, the mean red-urchin annual change is -21.65% using current visit-supported sizes and -22.66% using the broader pool. The corresponding purple-urchin means are -4.78% and -11.40%. These are unweighted means of separate history slopes, not pooled model estimates. At Point Vicente, the five-year purple-urchin slope changes from +2.37% to -7.37%; its uncertainty spans both directions under the independent-year diagnostic. None of these differences establishes a protection effect.

The [matched joint-model table](matched_joint_factorial.csv) is the current 2 x 2 comparison. It fits the same 30 PISCO urchin years under both size pools, using the integrated joint model. Two hybrid fits hold the measurement covariance from one size treatment fixed while using the annual biomass estimates from the other. Other taxa and programs are left as in the integrated review, so the all-program summaries still include ten red-urchin and ten purple-urchin biomass comparisons. These are model-level review results for the common cohort, not Emily's original pBACIPS model. The earlier independent-year/HAC `factorial_diagnostic.csv` is superseded for interpretation and retained only as a historical diagnostic.

For pooled purple-urchin biomass, the annual post-protection slope is -6.92% with current sizes and covariance. Switching to Emily's broader size pool while holding current covariance fixed gives -11.84%: a -4.92 percentage-point change in the point estimate. Holding current sizes and switching only the size-driven measurement covariance gives -6.50%: a +0.42-point change. With both archived size pool and its induced measurement covariance, the result is -11.78% (95% conditional interval -28.40% to +8.69%). The total -4.86-point difference is mostly associated with the biomass estimates, not the covariance change. The order of decomposition matters because the joint model re-estimates its weights and process variance. Red-urchin biomass shifts by only about -0.3 point across the two complete arms; its intervals span zero throughout.

The archived source file and site crosswalk are replayed for donor membership and nonlinear mass conversion, but the exact 1,000 draws from Emily's R script and her downstream pBACIPS model are not reproduced. Our archived-pool arm instead samples its source-linked sizes in the current integrated uncertainty model. These 30 common years are selected by joint support and do not represent the complete primary cohort. Coefficient uncertainty and unobserved LTER within-group size variation remain outside these intervals.

Files: `emily_style_pools.csv` records donor-pool summaries; `paired_annual_values.csv` gives every candidate year and the common-support flag; `same_year_descriptive_slopes.csv` gives point comparisons; `matched_joint_factorial.csv` gives the current model comparison; `factorial_diagnostic.csv` is a superseded illustrative check. Rebuild with `python3 code/tools/compare_emily_size_pool.py` from the analysis repository.
