# Seasonal and survey-footprint audit

Review only, November 1, 2026. The manuscript, supplement, canonical results and source datasets are unchanged.

The question is whether changing survey months or sampled habitat could look like recovery. We kept the researcher exclusions, all original paired years, designation clocks, baseline assignments, additive corrections and uncertainty method. A station/zone or transect was eligible for the fixed-footprint test only if it was observed throughout the years used by that history’s fitted models. Selection used survey presence, never response magnitude. No out-of-season surveys or additional years were added. The designation year is not fitted. For histories without a qualifying baseline, unused before-protection records do not restrict the post-only footprint; baseline-supported histories require a common footprint across both periods.

## What was checked and changed

PISCO: 100 of 103 response histories reproduce their archived annual raw values and log ratios. 78 permit a fixed station-and-zone footprint across all modeled years. KFM/LTER: 29 histories reproduce and permit fixed stations/transects. These counts include histories whose numerical values did not change. See correction_counts.csv for actual changes.

For PISCO invertebrate biomass, frozen simulated zone masses were used only where they reproduce archived values. This retains the existing shared-size-pool assumptions; it does not supply new zone-specific size samples. KFM benthic values use fixed stations because that processing averages replicates without a retained depth-zone unit. Fish and LTER comparisons use fixed transects where supported. All blocked or unaudited response values remain unchanged in the full-cohort sensitivity, so this is not a fully standardized replacement primary analysis.

The May–October swath filter removes some, but not all, zones in 52 mapped station-years within the current paired years, affecting 5 MPAs. This includes reference stations shared between MPAs, so these are MPA–station–year assignments, not necessarily independent surveys.

PISCO zone labels likewise do not prove matching actual depths between protected and reference sites.

## Remaining limits

- PISCO histories without a complete station/zone intersection cannot be fixed without changing years or estimating missing habitat. They remain flagged, not silently removed.
- Archived cache biomass does not reproduce the two single-year Point Dume SMCA urchin-biomass histories rebuilt in the earlier upstream review. Neither supplies a post-protection slope.
- Point Vicente kelp and Scorpion red-urchin density have explicit missing source measurements; this audit does not convert those unknowns to zeros. South Point sheephead biomass differs in reference-year 2005, where a positive count lacks length. These histories remain unchanged pending reconciliation. See reconstruction_checks.csv and missing_source_counts_or_sizes.csv.
- KFM Scorpion sheephead has no station/transect present throughout its full history; it is retained unchanged.
- Size-based KFM/LTER biomass and LTER lobster were audited for coverage but not fully reconstructed at fixed survey-unit resolution in this run. Shared size-pool and missing-length uncertainty remain separate issues.
- Changing the set of tested p-values can change a response’s adjusted q even when its estimate does not change. pooled.csv retains unadjusted p, BH q, effective degrees of freedom and inference flags.

## Recommendation

Use stable survey support as an explicit design requirement and retain these fixed-footprint results as a sensitivity while resolving incomplete footprints. Do not impose the PISCO seasonal cutoff on other programs or select the coverage rule by which intervals exclude zero. Keep short late histories separate from recovery-since-protection claims. The consistency check does not establish a causal cascade: before–after replication, habitat comparability and shared biomass sizes still limit that interpretation.

## Why the all-history lobster result changes

Point Dume SMCA contributes only 2021–2023 after protection. Its fixed-zone lobster log-ratio slope changes from −0.534 to −1.145 per year, with an estimated standard error of 0.104 rather than 0.231. That very short history therefore has greater influence after correction. A diagnostic omitting only this previously identified history gives a corrected pooled lobster trajectory of +8.7% per year (95% interval +1.2 to +16.7%). This is an influence check, not a proposed result-based exclusion. Its uncertainty remains difficult to estimate from three annual observations.

The LTER sheephead-biomass slopes also change markedly when fixed transects replace changing survey coverage: Campus Point −0.462 to +0.145 and Naples −0.257 to +0.230 log-ratio units/year. These are individual trajectories; the pooled sheephead trend remains uncertain. At Naples, the fixed reference transects have zero sheephead biomass in 2022 and 2023; reference transect AQUE 6, which lacks continuous coverage, contains positive biomass in those years. Its removal produces very large corrected ratios dependent on the retained additive constant. The fixed subset narrows the habitat represented and is not automatically a better population-wide estimate. This needs resolution before primary adoption.

Within the 140 early-start histories, corrected BH q-values are 0.041 for lobster density and both purple-urchin responses, 0.064 for kelp and 0.135 for red-urchin density. These do not justify choosing the cohort based on significance.
