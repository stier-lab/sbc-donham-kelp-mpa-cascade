Thiault2017 requires assessing stationarity of the Before protected/reference difference, additivity on the analysis scale, and temporal dependence. It does not supply our p-value, sample-size or equivalence cutoffs. Here annual log ratios represent the paired difference; only strictly prior observations enter baseline diagnostics. Positive and negative departures are treated identically. Post-effect magnitude or direction never decides eligibility.

Report shape/shift diagnostics separately; either p<.05 is a provisional flag, not an established ecological change-point. No selected break date search. Constant baselines and sparse baselines are unresolved for stability assessment.

This is an equivalence-style sensitivity grid, not Thiault's rule or a validated guarantee of stationarity.
