## Main findings

1. **No arithmetic failure or existing mechanical variance double-addition found.** Independent R verifiers passed: 3,208 calibration predictions, 428 interval checks, six borrowing slopes/HAC SEs and 18 saved-draw slopes; 34 joint-audit baseline slopes/HAC SEs, 306 saved-draw slopes and 296 KFM expectations. Independent Python reconstructs 624 measured-visit mass means from raw frequency records (maximum error 2.28e-13 g) and the 296 KFM expected biomass values (maximum error 9.10e-13). These establish arithmetic agreement, not calibration validity or total coverage.
2. **Temporal borrowing is much less validated than eight intervals imply.** Eight available other-visit validation rows are red urchin/lobster measurements at just two physical stations in 2015, with two reciprocal dates each (283/290-day gaps). There is no available purple-urchin temporal interval validation, although four purple-urchin visits use this borrowing method. Used temporal calibration pools have two or three site-years, two or three stations, and one or two years. Reverse-date residuals are algebraic ± pairs, not independent evidence; all 30 calibration site/year/zone groups have reciprocal residuals to 3.34e-16. Keep temporal borrowing exploratory; do not equate the computational two-group guard with adequate validation.
3. **The date guard checks only an upper bound.** Seven of eight other-visit borrowed uses and 12 of 131 cross-site uses have gaps below their corresponding calibration pool's minimum. Other-visit application gaps include 3, 108, 188 and 205 days, whereas its training gaps are 283–294 days. Shorter gaps need not be worse, but their error distribution is unvalidated. Amend R11/guide wording to “no larger than maximum calibration gap,” and expose minimum/maximum gaps and interpolation/extrapolation status. Do not impose a new minimum-gap exclusion without ecological justification.
4. **Dependence is only partly propagated.** Borrowed errors are shared within series/site/year/method, but independently redrawn across years and target sites. The actual application has 103 species/comparison/year/target-site pairs sharing at least one cross-site donor. Common donor errors are not coupled across those targets or to the donor's own measured-use draw. Cross-site calibration uses only three stations after outer exclusion at Abalone, eight at Vicente, repeatedly over years. Site-years are not independent station replication. Calibration-distribution uncertainty is fixed. Persisting station bias is not estimated by current draws.
5. **Local size bootstrap is conditional on measured values.** Of 624 measured visit uses, 84 have one measured animal and 95 have one observed size class (including the 84 singletons). Those 95 have zero within-sample Bayesian-bootstrap mass variance; this does not establish zero population uncertainty. Counts, allometry and offsets remain fixed in the six-history borrowing pipeline. Its 285 observed-zero count uses remain exactly zero in all draws; observation/detection uncertainty for zeros is outside scope.
6. **KFM visit clustering is mostly not estimable locally.** Raw 65,215 plant rows contain 18,413 exact duplicates; remaining 46,802 site/date/marker keys are unique. The five audited kelp histories use 296 distinct station-years: 220 have plant records (216 one-date, four two-date), 76 lack plant records but have zero count density. Of 191 positive-density station-years, one has a single measured plant (a-k-33, 2007). Current plant resampling is individual-plant within station-year, independent across years and count units; it cannot estimate common visit error from a single date. A 2,000-draw illustrative two-level weighting on the four two-date station-years increases conditional mass SD by about 1.45–2.82 times. This is a sensitivity, not an estimated correction or CI.

## Double-counting assessment

Current borrowed residuals compare two **measured sample means**. They contain donor sampling variation, target sampling variation and ecological transfer discrepancies. Current code does not add a separate donor bootstrap for borrowed predictions, and does not sum measurement and HAC variances. This restraint is correct. A future donor bootstrap plus the full existing residual would count donor sampling error twice; adding an independent target-size error also repeats target sampling already present. Summing HAC variance and measurement-draw variance is likewise unjustified because observed annual residuals already include measurement error. The present alternative also omits covariance with donor observations; absence of double addition does not make it a complete observation model.

## Minimal defensible next implementation (recommendation only)

1. **Immediate documentation-only change:** retain current conditional labels and add calibration station/year counts, reciprocal-pair count, temporal species-specific validation, gap support range, and unresolved dependence components. Keep all 63 density comparison-years and current missingness/identity holds. Do not promote biomass uncertainty or exclude additional histories based on these diagnostics.
2. **One coherent observation layer:** build an observation-ID registry on the current visit roster. Resample count units and directly measured sizes at verified physical units, sharing draws across every reuse and permanently identified unit through time. Couple count/size sampling where actual paired unit identifiers exist; otherwise document independence and sensitivity. Estimate observation covariance across annual ratios from these draws, including shared references. A singleton or all-identical empirical sample needs partial pooling/externally informed dispersion or an explicit uncertainty-unavailable flag, not an invented variance.
3. **Separate transfer from sample noise:** fit a small hierarchical latent log-mean-mass model to measured means with their sampling uncertainty, station effects, year/visit effects and appropriate species/role restrictions. Infer missing masses from the same model, so donor uncertainty enters once. Model the residual variance of observed/predicted calibration ratios with donor and target sampling covariance, rather than treating those ratios as independent true-population errors. With only three Abalone training stations and almost no temporal replication, fit/persistence estimates require pooling or declared sensitivity assumptions. Retain unsupported protected-site masses where exchangeability cannot be defended.
4. **Combine observation and process uncertainty once:** fit annual latent log ratios as trend plus calendar-time-correlated process variation, with known/estimated observation covariance from step 2 and transfer uncertainty from step 3. Estimate process variance conditional on that observation covariance (a small GLS/REML or hierarchical model), rather than using unadjusted HAC as a second variance component. Use a joint parametric bootstrap/posterior simulation with refitting to propagate fitted variance/calibration parameters. Preserve shared-station/reference covariance across comparisons. Short five-to-ten-year series cannot reliably estimate a free AR parameter per comparison: pool cautiously and show fixed-correlation alternatives. This is a proposed implementation, not an analysis run in this audit.
5. **Treat coefficients and offsets explicitly:** use shared species/month allometric parameter draws only if coefficient covariance or original calibration data can be recovered. Monthly kelp file supplies slope/intercept/p-value/R²/N but no covariance matrix or calibration residual distribution. Do not fabricate independent coefficient errors. Offsets are fixed transformation choices (reconstructed from inherited ratios), not automatically random parameters: predeclare plausible offset sensitivity and re-estimate only if the chosen offset rule is data-derived and intended to be recalculated. Report conditional intervals plus scenario variation if source uncertainty remains unavailable; do not label them unconditional “total uncertainty.”
