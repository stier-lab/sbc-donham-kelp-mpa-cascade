# Protection scope checked November 1, 2026

All four named areas meet the frozen scope: at least one focal predator protected from ordinary recreational and commercial take. Point Dume SMR and SMCA are separate areas, each designated in 2012. Anacapa SMCA and Painted Cave use their 2003 protection year.

Anacapa SMCA permits recreational and commercial lobster take, but protects sheephead. Painted Cave permits recreational lobster and pelagic fish take, but protects sheephead. Neither is a no-take reserve or a full lobster refuge. Point Dume SMR prohibits take; the adjacent SMCA exceptions are white seabass/pelagic fisheries, not sheephead or lobster.

Keep these differences attached to the comparisons. A pooled association from this scope is not a single estimate of lobster fishing prohibition. Adding all usable focal responses under the same site-level scope does not imply all focal taxa share identical protection.
