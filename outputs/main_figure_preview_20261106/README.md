# Main figure previews from the integrated review

These five figures show how the main figure sequence could look using the corrected, integrated review data. They are previews for choosing the display. They do not replace the manuscript figures or its frozen numerical results. Rebuild them with `python3 code/tools/preview_main_figures.py`. The input hashes are in `input_manifest.json`.

- **Figure 1, site map.** The 22 protected areas represented in the annual review are shown as current polygons. Five sites are labeled to orient the case histories and kelp comparison. The dots mark polygon centroids. They are not survey stations or reference sites, and the current polygons do not establish historical boundaries.
- **Figure 2, three case histories.** Scorpion, Harris Point and Naples show annual protected/reference log ratios from supported observations. Orange is lobster, blue sheephead, purple purple urchin, rust red urchin and green giant kelp. Each survey program has its own line where more than one program contributes. The vertical dashed line marks the designation year. These are observed histories, without fitted trends or uncertainty intervals.
- **Figure 3, separate questions.** Panel a shows the average post-protection difference from the observed pre-protection baseline for eligible histories. Panel b shows the post-protection annual trend. Small points are individual history estimates. Large points and bars are pooled estimates and 95% intervals from the integrated review. An empty row has no eligible before–after estimate; it is not a zero. These panels answer different questions and must not be combined into one effect size.
- **Figure 4, observed biomass through time.** Thin lines show supported biomass histories after designation. The thick line is the median of available histories at each elapsed year, drawn only where at least three histories contribute. It is a descriptive summary, not a fitted recovery curve or uncertainty interval. The number and identity of histories vary through time. The restricted KFM sheephead biomass branch is not part of this full-history display.
- **Figure 5, paired kelp change.** Blue is protected and orange reference. Each point is kelp biomass relative to that side's own 2010–2013 baseline. Rows retain program identity, so two rows for an MPA are not independent protected areas. A blank row in one period has no supported pair for that period. The dashed vertical line marks no change from baseline. Large ratios for Anacapa KFM and Santa Barbara Island KFM arise in part from small baselines and should not be read as strong protection effects on their own. This preview shows diver-program biomass pairs; it does not reproduce the full historical heatwave or satellite resilience analysis in the current paper.

The integrated review still needs a final choice of estimands, downstream uncertainty and site/reference checks before any of these previews can replace a manuscript figure. The plotted values for Figures 2 and 5 are saved beside the images for direct checking.
