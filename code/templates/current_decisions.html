How we decide what to use

A site can meet the site rules but still have too few usable years. Each year is checked separately for surveys and, when needed, sizes for biomass.

What we compare: When we have enough earlier years, we ask whether the difference between protected and reference sites changed after designation and how it changed later. Otherwise, we describe the later trend only. These patterns alone cannot show that protection caused the change. The designation date in our records may differ from the date protection first began.
