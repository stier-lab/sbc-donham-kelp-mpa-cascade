# PISCO routing, clocks and Campus Point: fixed-cohort comparison

29 October 2026. Exploratory alternatives to the frozen V44 analysis. Primary data, selected results, manuscript and supplement have not been changed by this comparison.

## What was held fixed

Every scenario retains the same 145 MPA–program–species–response histories and all 2,827 annual observations. Each history retains exactly the same annual inside/outside values, source mapping and observed years. No new reference is selected and no series is dropped. Before/after labels change only in the explicitly named boundary scenario. All six trophic regressions keep their original MPA memberships. Coefficient inputs remain the Li-confirmed SBC127.5 Table 2 Model 1 release.

This is a comparison of analytical choices, not a new test establishing causal effects of protection. The synthesis still combines different kinds of contrasts; its estimates must not be presented as a common fold increase caused by protection.

## PISCO eligibility

The frozen V6 methods specify CI for fewer than three pre-implementation samples. Current annual paired records contain 22 PISCO histories with at least three years labeled Before. Ten reach three only because the designation year, 2012, is included. Requiring years strictly earlier than designation leaves 12.

A before-trend screen leaves 20 histories eligible under the original boundary and 11 under the strict boundary. Point Vicente red-urchin density has a detectable prior trend (p=0.0466 under the original boundary). Campus Point PISCO lobster density has a constant zero log ratio in all three before-years; both inside and reference raw annual densities are zero. Its trend test is undefined. The comparison conservatively retains CI for that history rather than treating an undefined test as evidence of parallel trends. This conservative handling is our explicit sensitivity rule, not a recovered instruction from Emily.

Each eligible history was fit with Step, Linear, Asymptotic and Sigmoid candidates on the same observations. Only converged genuine nonlinear fits with finite covariance were eligible. Selection used AICc; selected models were all Step or Linear. The original-boundary three-year scenario selects eight Step and twelve Linear fits. Eligibility changes use the same supplied calendar clock as the existing PISCO analyses. Testing before-trends against observation index instead of calendar year changes no PISCO eligibility decision in this cohort.

We also required four or five before-years. Those scenarios reroute 11 and 5 histories under the original boundary, or 5 and 2 under the strict boundary. Requiring three before-years for every available species/response at an MPA reroutes none: each PISCO MPA has at least one retained response with fewer than three. That is compatible with the blanket CI choice, but does not prove it was the intended rule. Counting annual records alone cannot establish adequate transect coverage or comparability of early surveys.

A separate scenario applies strict eligibility but preserves the original Before/After labels. This separates changing eligibility from assigning the designation-year observation to After. It is identical to the original-boundary four-year scenario in this particular cohort; that equivalence is not assumed for future datasets.

All nine pooled directions stay the same across the 14 scenarios. Higher kelp biomass, higher sheephead biomass and lower purple-urchin density remain supported. Under the three-year PISCO rerouting, red-urchin biomass and purple-urchin biomass no longer meet the adjusted 0.05 threshold with the original uncertainty calculation. Lobster density gains support under the original boundary but not the strict boundary. We should not turn that into a new general lobster claim.

Calendar-gap HAC sensitivity uses the same histories and models, with uncertainty no smaller than conditional IID uncertainty for each history. Because changing variances changes meta-analysis weights and fitted heterogeneity, pooled intervals need not widen monotonically. With HAC, purple-urchin biomass retains support in these routing scenarios; red-urchin biomass retains support under the original boundary (q=0.044) but not the strict boundary (q=0.054). Therefore the urchin biomass conclusions depend on both routing and uncertainty choices. Do not select an uncertainty method according to which result crosses 0.05.

## Clocks and prediction windows

First, the retained model family was fixed and observation-index clocks were replaced with elapsed calendar years, with pre-designation time clipped at zero as in the existing calendar fits. At the existing 0–11 endpoint, all nine pooled directions and adjusted decisions remain unchanged. This isolates the clock change, but is not a recommendation to retain extrapolated endpoints.

The original 0–11 contrasts extend outside the observed time range for 39 histories: 24 start before the observed clock range and 15 end beyond it. Switching clocks alone leaves 34 contrasts outside the observed calendar range. These flags describe prediction support, not missing annual data inserted into a fit.

Next, both native-clock and calendar-clock fits were evaluated at the exact same observed calendar endpoints for each history: its first After survey and its latest surveyed year no more than 11 calendar years later. Both fits used all the same observations. Mean and Step estimands were unchanged. Durations can differ across histories and are recorded; this does not create a common 11-year causal estimand.

Changing the clock on these matched observed windows changes no pooled direction or adjusted decision under either uncertainty calculation. Changing the window relative to the original 0–11 contrasts weakens red-urchin biomass support with the original uncertainty (q=0.080 for calendar windows); HAC retains it narrowly (q=0.047). This is a window/uncertainty issue, not evidence that the calendar clock alone reverses a result.

## Campus Point lobster biomass

The history spans 2012–2023. Its only record labeled Before is 2012; there is no observation strictly before the 2012 designation year. The original script explicitly records this as CI. The current helper instead writes pBACIPS metadata while fitting the same supplied-time linear trend. The label is unsupported and should be corrected to CI in the next coordinated rebuild.

A label-only correction changes no estimate, interval, pooled result or trophic regression. The retained trend contrast is 1.656 (SE 0.701). Restricting the contrast to observed After years 2013–2023 gives 1.506 (SE 0.637), simply a ten-year rather than eleven-year trend contrast. These are changes in an inside/outside difference, not before/after evidence of a protection effect.

For context only, replacing the trend contrast with a mean-level CI estimate gives 0.117 (SE 0.262). That answers a different biological question and is not a correction implied by relabeling the model. It should not be substituted automatically.

## Trophic conclusions

The four-MPA lobster-biomass/purple-urchin-biomass slope remains negative after the three-year PISCO rerouting, but its nominal p changes from 0.0397 to 0.1316. The point slope barely changes (−0.5616 to −0.5643); its uncertainty increases. The existing shared-reference and small-sample cautions still apply. The other relationships remain uncertain, and one uncertain sheephead-density slope changes sign. No scenario establishes a complete predator–urchin–kelp cascade.

Clock changes at matched observed endpoints do not change the nominal decisions for the six trophic regressions. The alternative Campus Point mean-level estimand also weakens the four-MPA lobster association (p=0.312); again, that substitutes a different estimand and is not the label-only correction.

## Decision and next step

Keep these as explicit sensitivity comparisons while the intended eligibility rule and designation-year treatment are clarified. The core kelp/sheephead/purple-urchin-density pattern persists. The urchin biomass and lobster pathway claims need their existing caution, not stronger wording. Correct Campus Point's label in the next coordinated pipeline/manuscript rebuild, without silently replacing its trend estimator with a mean.

Reproduce with `Rscript code/R/52_routing_clock_sensitivity.R`, then `python3 code/tests/check_routing_clock_sensitivity.py`. Both the full pipeline and review-check runner now call module 52. Tables use the prefix `table_s_routing_clock_`. The R run reconstructs every retained contrast and reproduces Tables 2 and 3 before comparing alternatives. The independent Python check reconstructs all 142 Mean/Step/Linear primary contrasts and their SEs; the three nonlinear contrasts are verified by the R reconstruction. Input hashes and fixed membership checks pass. No failed candidate or clock fit was silently omitted.
