# Satellite siting diagnostic

Specified before computing the new contrasts, 23 October 2026. Retain the
primary Landsat pair roster without selecting on trends or effect sizes.
Use the existing annual kelp index and recorded offsets; do not change masks,
reference assignments, primary fits or manuscript results.

Pre-designation descriptions use all available years from 1984, the last ten
years and the last five years, excluding the designation year. Report starting
levels, zeros, ordinary least-squares log-contrast slopes, raw index tracking and
linearly detrended tracking. Slopes/correlations are descriptive, with no
annual-independent P values or extrapolated counterfactuals.

The primary local change compares five years before with five years after an
anchor year, excluding the anchor. Require at least 80% paired annual coverage
on each side. Sensitivities use three and seven years. For width w, placebo
anchors are designation minus (w+1) and minus (2w+1), ensuring their follow-up
ends strictly before designation. Keep the intersection of pairs eligible at
all three anchors fixed within each width. Export exclusions and exact years.
Different widths may retain different cohorts; do not treat their differences
as window effects alone.

Report mean annual shifted-log contrasts, unshifted index differences and
log ratios of period means (only where all four means are positive). The latter
has a separately recorded fixed positive-mean cohort. Do not convert the index
into physical biomass or remove zeros for log convenience. Pooled summaries
weight pairs equally; placebo comparisons are descriptive, not randomization
tests, and no P values or causal confidence intervals are computed. Actual and
placebo windows have different environmental histories. Shared-reference
overlap remains unresolved and forbids an independence claim.

Earlier-disturbance information is taken from module 28's historical/modern
comparison on identical endpoint-specific pairs, not presented as a new test.
The 1997-1998 event may overlap placebo windows; placebo outcomes are therefore
not independent of the historical-event evidence.
