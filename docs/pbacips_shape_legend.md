# Figure S2: Retained sigmoidal histories and same-data linear comparators

All three retained sigmoidal pBACIPS histories are shown: (A) lobster
(*Panulirus interruptus*) density at Gull Island SMR, (B) red-urchin
(*Mesocentrotus franciscanus*) biomass at Harris Point SMR, and (C) giant-kelp
(*Macrocystis pyrifera*) biomass at Scorpion SMR. All three use KFM annual
paired observations. Blue circles denote Before observations and orange
triangles After observations. Solid black curves reproduce the retained
sigmoidal fits; dashed green curves show unselected linear comparators
fitted to the same data, not winners of a new model-selection analysis.
Axes show calendar years and unitless annual lnRR, with the primary input's
normalization and zero correction unchanged. Dotted vertical separators
are placed at designation year + 0.5 to distinguish annual Before and
After observations; they do not specify an exact designation date.

The fitted clock is the original observation index: all Before observations
and the first After observation have model time zero, and subsequent After
observations are numbered sequentially. For display on a calendar axis,
this clock is interpolated between observed After years; it is not replaced
by a calendar-time model. Each reproduced zero-to-11 contrast matches its
primary exported Mean within 1e-7. Curves are drawn only within observed
calendar-year support. No confidence or prediction intervals are shown;
the descriptive shapes do not establish a realized biological plateau,
account for model-selection uncertainty or demonstrate causal protection
effects. Supporting Atlas B displays all 36 retained pBACIPS histories
(27 Linear, 6 Step, 3 Sigmoid); its membership ledger also records all 144
primary keys and distinguishes the 108 CI histories outside this display.
In the observed record, panel A's retained curve is still rising, panel B's
curve decreases toward a lower level, and panel C's curve levels off after
a steep increase. The retained Sigmoid label therefore does not mean that
all three histories show an observed plateau.

# Supporting Atlas B: Every retained pBACIPS fit

The nine plot pages show all 36 retained pBACIPS source-specific histories,
four lettered panels per page. Axes identify the MPA, taxon, response and
source. Blue circles denote annual Before lnRR observations and orange
triangles After observations. Solid black lines reproduce the retained
model, named at the right endpoint. Dotted separators at designation
year + 0.5 divide the annual Before and After categories rather than
marking an exact event date. Step models change between those categories.
Progressive models use the original observation-index clock, including
zero for the first After observation, except Landsat linear histories
and Campus Point LTER lobster biomass, which retain script 08's supplied
calendar-years-since-designation `time` route. Index-clock curves interpolate
the native index between observed After years for calendar-axis display;
supplied-calendar curves use elapsed calendar time directly. Neither route
changes the primary fitting clock. The observation and audit exports identify both clocks,
missing years and any 11-unit contrast horizon beyond observed model time.

This is a retained-fit display, not a graph of every candidate AICc fit or
a repeated model-selection exercise. Retained labels are taken from the
current primary table; no history is chosen for its visual appearance.
All reproduced primary contrasts must match the exported Mean within
1e-7 before the figure cache can be replaced. Curves are descriptive,
without confidence or prediction intervals. The index links each page
and panel to its exact MPA-taxon-source-response primary key. The parent
adds the title/index pages and final publication cross-references.
