# Retention and within-site sampling checks

23 October 2026. Module 49 supplements modules 47-48; it does not replace them.

Retention: use the primary-year, BOT+MID, 80%-core selection separately for
post-trend and before/after endpoints. For each excluded site, compare its first
three available overlapping years with retained sites of the same MPA and
protection status. Freeze the retained comparator roster across those years.
Also export an all-overlapping-years comparison. Require actual observations;
never fill missing surveys. Report how many comparisons have pre-designation
observations, and do not describe post-designation observations as an untreated
baseline. Compare mean species log(count + 0.5) within targeted and warm-affinity
non-targeted panels, and surveyed bottom depth. These are descriptive contrasts,
not independent replicated tests: sites and reference reefs can be reused.
Cannot infer why a site was discontinued or equate a shorter record with site
abandonment. Late-starting sites may also be excluded by coverage.

Within-site consistency: retain each eligible module-48 site's exact identity.
Select zones present in every original retained year at that site. If any site
has no common zone, the pair is unavailable; do not replace or discard that site.
Reconstruct standard BOT+MID densities from the same valid cells as module 47.
First use fixed zones alone. Then restrict BOT transects to within 2 m of the
site-zone median recorded bottom depth across the original years; 1 and 3 m are
sensitivity ranges. Medians and zone selection use sampling metadata, not fish
responses. Missing bottom depths are excluded only from depth-restricted fits.
Retain MID observations only at visits/zones with retained BOT observations.
VRG MID depth is mostly missing: this constrains bottom depth, not both vertical
strata. No interpretation of zone codes as equivalent depths among reefs.

Each fixed zone has equal weight across years. Retain only years with every
selected zone at every original core site; do not change the roster as years
drop. Preserve the previous eligibility thresholds and fit methods. Compare
each restriction to the original calculation on precisely the same sites and
years. Report both coverage loss and effects; no selection by sign or P value.
Use the same species panel, size variants, 0.5-fish offset, calendar-gap HAC lag 2
and equal-MPA delete-shared-site-group summaries. Save all observations, rosters,
depth centers, omissions, fits and checksums. No full pipeline or manuscript
rebuild is needed for these optional diagnostics.

Metadata audit: no conflicting nonmissing depths within survey cells were found
in the eligible raw-site/method frame. Canopy cover is entirely missing there;
substrate and exposure are not supplied by this fish table. These checks cannot
establish habitat equivalence or distinguish siting from protection causally.
