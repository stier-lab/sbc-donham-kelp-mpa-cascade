# Figure 5: giant-kelp trajectories and paired disturbance comparisons

Generated by `code/R/22_resistance_recovery.R` with

Only panel letters appear above the plots; no titles or subtitles.

## Caption

Figure 5. Giant-kelp biomass remained higher inside MPAs during and after the
2014-2016 marine heatwave. (A) Annual mean diver-derived dry foliar biomass
inside MPAs (blue) and paired reference reefs (orange); ribbons show +/- 1 SE
among contributing MPA-status-year values. The shaded band marks the heatwave.
KFM, LTER and PISCO sources were averaged within each MPA-status-year;
Landsat is not included. Annual coverage varies from 9 to 11 pairs, with the
same pair membership inside and outside each year. (B) Each point is one pair's
MPA/reference ratio of biomass relative to its separate 2010-2013 baselines.
Blue indicates ratios above 1 and orange below 1; the dashed line marks parity.
Diamonds show geometric means and bars show 95% t intervals on log ratios,
back-transformed to the ratio scale. These intervals are not intervals for the
paired Wilcoxon tests reported in Results. Each period includes 10 eligible
pairs, but membership differs: Harris Point contributes during 2014-2016 and
Matlahuayl replaces it in the later periods.

Panel A remains a descriptive available-pair trajectory, not a fixed-cohort
estimate. Period-specific eligibility additionally requires positive finite
baseline and period means for both statuses. Harris Point's later-period kelp
ratios do not pass that requirement; Matlahuayl's heatwave ratio does not pass.
No inclusion decision or numerical result was changed for this figure.

Panel B uses full-precision values before the existing paired table rounds its
inside/outside ratios. Summary exports are
`tables/table_figure5_period_pairs.csv` and
`tables/table_figure5_period_summary.csv`; their rounded estimates and intervals
match the primary-baseline sensitivity table. The three geometric means are
4.22, 5.30 and 5.02, distinct from the separate median inside/outside ratios
reported in Results.
